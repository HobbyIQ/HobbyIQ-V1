## How to reproduce

Read-only, against prod `card_catalog`. Group hobbymonitor rows by (sport, cardYear, setKey); for each
cell, read every row of that cell, index the non-hobbymonitor strict rows by their slug segments
`4|5|6` (cardNumber, parallelSlug, auto), and classify each hobbymonitor row: a rival at the same cell
with a different player is `contradicted`; a rival that agrees (or carries no name) is `corroborated`;
no rival at all is `single-source`. Graded children are excluded from the rival set — a row minted
from its parent cannot confirm it.
